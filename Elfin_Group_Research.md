## 7. Observations and gaps

1. **The website is down.** It is the biggest gap in their digital presence, and captions still point people to it.
2. **Small social reach:** 390 IG followers and 17 FB followers for a company with a 15-year history and a "1000+ families" claim.
3. **RERA claims aren't backed by visible numbers.** Hashtags say "RERA Approved", but no K-RERA registration number appears in any post or listing found. Check https://rera.karnataka.gov.in before relying on it.
4. **Unit and floor counts differ.** The website says Nebula is 8 floors with 112 units, while the poster render looks taller. Emerald Tower may account for the difference.
5. **Only 12 of 34 posts** can be read without an Instagram login. The other 22 older posts weren't reviewed.
