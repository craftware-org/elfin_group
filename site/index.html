About Elfin Group

Shaping real estate since 2008.

Elfin Group is a team of builders, developers and promoters from Hubballi. From apartments to well-planned plotted layouts, we create spaces people are proud to call home.

Every project follows one simple rule: no compromises, whether it is the plan, the materials or the finish. Our company, Elfin Infra Builders Pvt. Ltd., has its office at Elfin Castle in Keshwapur.
