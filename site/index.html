Specifications

What goes into every Elfin Nebula home, as listed in the project brochure.

Flooring
2' × 2' vitrified DC flooring in all the rooms.
Kitchen
Granite kitchen platform with branded SS sink.
Painting
Acrylic emulsion paints for external wall.
Electrical
Concealed copper wiring of Finolex make with MCBs. Provision for TV & AC point.
Doors
Decorative laminated doors with wooden frames. Laminated doors in toilet.
Windows
Large sliding windows in plain / frosted glass in powder coated / anodized aluminium with safety grills, to ensure ample sunlight throughout the day.
Lifts
2 bed lifts and 2 passenger lifts.
Water supply
Underground & overhead water tank with adequate storage capacity. Special water proofing treatment with China chips flooring on top floor. Solar water plumbing provided to each flat.
